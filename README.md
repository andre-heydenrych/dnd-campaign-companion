# dnd-campaign-companion

Player-side companion for our D&D campaigns: a shared lore wiki, maps, a shop/item tracker and a house-rule reference — used at (and between) sessions by me and my players across two ongoing campaigns.

## Why
Our campaigns generate a lot of lore, shops and house rules that currently live in scattered notes and chat. This app gives the party one place to record what we've learned and what we can buy.

## Planned features
### Lore wiki
- [ ] NPCs, places, factions, deities and history pages with links between them
- [ ] Tags and full-text search
- [ ] "What the party knows" — rumours vs confirmed facts, updated as we learn more
- [ ] Campaign timeline and session notes that feed into lore

### Maps
- [ ] Upload maps and pin locations linked to lore pages

### Shops & items
- [ ] Shops per town with shopkeeper (linked NPC), inventory and prices
- [ ] Item catalogue: SRD items plus homebrew, with house-rule pricing
- [ ] Party gold and purchase log

### House rules
- [ ] Searchable house-rule reference per campaign

### Platform
- [ ] Multiple campaigns, player accounts and permissions
- [ ] Mobile-friendly for use at the table
- [ ] Markdown import/export

## Structure
- `api/` — backend
- `web/` — frontend
